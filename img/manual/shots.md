# Manual screenshots

Generated by `tools/docshots/run.mjs` (see `tools/docshots/README.md`). Every picture exists twice with the same file name:
`img/manual/ja/<name>.png` (Japanese UI) and `img/manual/en/<name>.png` (English UI). Demo data only; nothing private.

Full pictures are the application client area at 1120x720 px (no window frame); the two phone pictures are the phone page at 390x844 px.
Crops are cut from a full picture and are marked "crop". Numbered call-outs are filled circles (#e8743b, white ring, white bold digit)
baked into the image; the digits are the same in both languages and are explained per picture below. The pictures contain no other
annotation text. Everything shown is demo data on a fixed date (2026-09-18).
